How this works — and what it never does: Deduction Desk runs entirely from files you export yourself (your POs, invoices, shipment records, and the deduction notices you received). We never ask for your Retail Link login, we never sign in as you, and we never submit disputes for you — Walmart's own vendor rules terminate supplier access for credential sharing, so the finished packet is yours to upload. Your data stays on your machine.
